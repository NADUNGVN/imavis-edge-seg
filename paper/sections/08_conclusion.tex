\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The current evidence supports a cautious conclusion: one compiler-safe elastic segmentation supernet can provide several static levels that compile and run on two materially different edge backends, and a measured-latency lookup selects different levels for the same budget. The shared model passes the first augmented parity gate against a comparable Fast-SCNN baseline, while adverse-condition behavior demonstrates why per-condition and per-class analysis is necessary.

This is not yet a complete claim of hardware-aware optimization or calibrated reliability. A final conclusion requires three-seed headline numbers, FLOPs-aware and energy-aware comparisons, QAT, a working calibrated router, the remaining ablations, and the systematic literature review.
