\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

This section is intentionally a structured placeholder until the planned systematic literature review is complete. The review will cover four gaps: (i) lightweight and real-time segmentation, including the disconnect between FLOPs and measured latency; (ii) compiler portability across TensorRT, Xavier DLA, and Hailo-style dataflow NPUs; (iii) adverse-condition segmentation and uncertainty-aware evaluation, including ACDC; and (iv) dynamic, once-for-all, and elastic supernets that avoid independently training every deployment budget.

Adjacent systems such as YOLIC, Light-SEF, UCPNet, and HARD should be positioned against the specific combination targeted here rather than against any single axis. The final version must report a method-versus-gap table and cite primary sources for each comparison.
